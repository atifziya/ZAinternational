Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ZA International Tours & Travels. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information when you visit our website or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide seamless travel and documentation services, we may collect:

  • Personal Identity (Name, Passport details for visa processing).
  • Contact Information (Email, Phone number, Address).
  • Travel Preferences and Pilgrimage requirements.
  • Payment details for booking tickets or packages.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly for:

  • Processing Visas, Passports, and Flight bookings.
  • Managing Haj and Umrah pilgrimage arrangements.
  • Communicating updates regarding your travel status.
  • Improving our customer service and website experience.

4. Data Security

We implement high-level security measures to ensure that your sensitive documents (like Passports and ID proofs) are protected from unauthorized access. We do not sell or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

For bookings and legalizations, we may share necessary data with airlines, embassies, and government departments. These parties are obligated to handle your data securely and solely for the requested service.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you or ask for corrections. If you wish to delete your data from our records, you can contact our management team.
